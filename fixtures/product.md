# Quillpath (demo product, fictional)

**One line:** Quillpath is an AI pipeline-review assistant for early B2B sales teams. It reads
your CRM every night and tells the team which deals are stalling, why, and what to do next.

## Who it is for
- B2B software companies at Seed to Series B, roughly 20–200 employees.
- Sales is still founder-led or run by a first sales leader, with no full-time RevOps person.
- Teams that just raised or are hiring their first account executives and need a repeatable
  pipeline process before the new reps start.

## Buyers
- **Champion:** Head of Sales, VP Sales, Head of Revenue Operations, first sales leader.
- **Economic buyer:** CEO / founder, or CRO where one exists.

## What it does
- Connects to HubSpot or Salesforce in about 15 minutes (read-only access).
- Flags stalled deals every morning: no activity, missing next step, or close date slipped.
- Writes a weekly pipeline and forecast summary the founder can forward to the board.
- Gives each rep a short list of next actions per deal.

## Why now (typical triggers)
- **New funding round:** the board expects a forecast, and new reps are being hired.
- **Sales hiring:** onboarding AEs into a pipeline process that does not exist yet.
- **Headcount growth:** more deals than the founder can personally review.
- **New sales leader:** they want visibility into the pipeline in their first 90 days.

## Proof points you may cite
- Setup takes about 15 minutes; no RevOps project needed.
- Works on top of the existing CRM; nothing to migrate.
- Free 14-day pilot on the team's real pipeline.
